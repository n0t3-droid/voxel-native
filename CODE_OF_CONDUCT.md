# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Voxel Native welcomes rigorous, respectful collaboration. Discuss evidence and
design decisions directly; treat people with patience, assume good faith, and
make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Constructive participation includes:

- giving specific, technically grounded feedback;
- separating observed behavior from inference;
- acknowledging uncertainty, limitations, and prior work;
- protecting private data and following repository safety boundaries;
- accepting a maintainer's decision to defer or reject work that lacks evidence
  or exceeds a declared budget.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets or
vulnerabilities, sustained disruption, and retaliation against a good-faith
reporter.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and project-related public interactions. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close contributions, restrict participation, or ban accounts when behavior puts
people or the project at risk.

Use GitHub's reporting and blocking controls for immediate platform abuse. For a
maintainer conduct concern, request a private contact channel without posting
incident details publicly. Reports will be handled as privately as the available
GitHub tools permit; conflicts of interest should be disclosed and avoided.
